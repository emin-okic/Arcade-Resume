# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Arcade Resume is a personal resume app presented as an arcade-style game. This policy explains what information the app collects, uses, and shares.

## Information We Collect

Arcade Resume does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not require an account, does not ask you to enter personal details, and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, camera input, or files from your device.

## Gameplay and Device Interactions

Arcade Resume processes gameplay input, such as touch gestures, keyboard input, character selection, score, progress, and reset actions, locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game experience during your session.

The app may use local audio playback and haptic feedback to provide arcade-style sound and vibration effects. It does not record audio and does not access the microphone.

## Resume Content

The resume details shown in the app are built into the app. Viewing this content does not send information to the developer or to any third party.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Arcade Resume does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

## Data Sharing

Because Arcade Resume does not collect personal information, it does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage and Retention

Arcade Resume does not store user personal information on external servers. Any temporary gameplay state is handled locally by the app and is not retained for user tracking.

## Children’s Privacy

Arcade Resume is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app changes in a way that affects privacy practices. Any updated policy should include a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact information provided in the app listing or accompanying resume materials.
